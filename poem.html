Four Years Apart, Still Us ❤️

Four years apart, yet somehow still together,
holding on through every change in weather.
But our story began long before these years,
back when we were kids, before these hopes and fears.

Not everyone believes in where this road will end,
and we've heard the doubts from family and friends.
We've faced the distance, criticism, and pain,
yet somehow we keep choosing us again.

And through it all, we've put our trust in God,
even when the path ahead felt long and hard.
Maybe we don't know exactly what comes next,
but I know whose hand I want to hold through it.

Because I loved you when I first decided
that one day you were the woman I wanted to marry.
I love you now, after everything we've been through,
and somehow every year makes me more certain of you.

We're not there yet, but we're getting so close.
Closer to the life we've talked about the most.
No more counting miles or wishing you were near,
just waking up together year after year.

And I'm impatient—I won't even pretend.
I want our home, our family, and everything ahead.
I want to spoil you more than you'll ever let me,
and spend my life finding new ways to make you happy.

We've already made it through so much apart.
Imagine what we'll do when distance isn't part
of the story anymore—just you and me, together,
finally beginning the rest of our forever. ❤️
